# Code of Conduct

Debate runs on good faith: we disagree hard about ideas while treating people
with respect. This project works the same way.

## Our standards

We expect everyone taking part (in issues, pull requests, reviews and
discussions) to:

- Be welcoming and patient, including with newcomers and non-native English speakers.
- Critique code and ideas, never people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before concluding otherwise.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give it constructively.

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers of The Studio of Sadiq may remove, edit or reject comments,
commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this code, and
may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they judge
harmful.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the
organisation's GitHub page. Reports are handled confidentially.
