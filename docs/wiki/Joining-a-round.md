# Debater and timekeeper guide

## Joining

1. Connect to the same Wi-Fi or hotspot as the chair.
2. Open the app → **Join**. Enter your name and pick a role. Debaters also pick
   their seat (the chair can change it).
3. Tap **Scan QR code** and point it at the chair's screen, or type the line
   shown under the QR (for example `192.168.43.1 Y2BK3M`).

   You can also scan the chair's QR with your phone's normal camera app: it
   opens the join screen with the code filled in.
4. Wait for the chair to approve you. Your screen then shows the live timer,
   with *synced* and the delay to the chair's device in the top bar.

If the connection drops, the app reconnects on its own and you keep your role.

## As a debater

- Your phone shows **POI as …**, **POO as …** and **POP as …** buttons for your
  seat. POIs only work during the open window and from the other side of the
  house.
- When it's your speech, the top line says *You have the floor* and POIs
  offered to you appear with **Accept** / **Decline**.
- You can **Withdraw** your own point while it's pending.
- **Recording:** once your POI is accepted (or while your POO/POP is being
  heard), your phone records you and sends the clip to the chair. Allow
  microphone access when asked; the app asks when you join, so it won't
  interrupt you mid-point.

## As a timekeeper

You get the same timer controls as the chair: **Start**, **Pause**, **Resume**,
**Stop**, **Next →**, and **Ring bell**. Rulings on points stay with the chair.

## As a wing adjudicator or viewer

You see the synced timer and hear the bells. Notes and ballots are kept on the
chair's device only.
