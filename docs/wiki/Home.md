# Debate Adjudicator docs

Debate Adjudicator runs a debate room from the chair adjudicator's phone or
laptop: timer and bells, devices joined over local Wi-Fi, interruption
handling, recordings, transcription, ballots and a PDF report.

## Guides

- [Chair guide](Chair-guide.md): set up a round, invite devices, run speeches,
  rule on points, finish with transcripts, ballot and report.
- [Debater and timekeeper guide](Joining-a-round.md): join from your phone, raise
  POIs and points, keep time.
- [Transcription](Transcription.md): offline models, cloud providers, what to expect.
- [Ballots and reports](Ballots-and-reports.md): scoring rules for BP, UADC and WSDC,
  and what goes in the PDF.
- [Troubleshooting](Troubleshooting.md): devices can't connect, bells are late,
  recording or scanning problems.

## Contributing

- [Translating the app](Translating.md): add your language in one command,
  then translate a single file.
- [Adding a debate format](Adding-a-format.md): describe a format's speeches,
  timing, teams and scoring in one short file.
