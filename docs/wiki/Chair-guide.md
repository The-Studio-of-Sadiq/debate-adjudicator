# Chair guide

The chair adjudicator's device is the **host**: it holds the round, decides
what every other device may do, and keeps the private material (notes,
ballot, transcripts).

## Before the round

1. **Pick the network.** Venue Wi-Fi often stops devices from seeing each
   other. The most reliable setup is your phone's **hotspot**, with everyone
   joining it. In the lobby, **Wi-Fi QR** shows a code people can scan with
   their camera to join your hotspot.
2. **Check the bells.** Home → **Bell settings** changes speech length, grace
   period, POI window and each bell (time, pattern, label). Settings are saved
   per format.
3. **Download a transcription model** on Wi-Fi if you'll transcribe offline:
   Home → **Transcription settings** → Download next to **Base**.

## Start and invite

1. Home → choose **British Parliamentary** or **UADC**, enter the motion,
   **Start round**.
2. Tap **Invite devices**. Show the QR code, or read out the line under it
   (for example `192.168.43.1 Y2BK3M`).
3. Requests appear under **Requests**. Check or change the role and seat, then
   **Approve**. Approving a debater for a seat fills in their name for the
   ballot and report.
4. You can change anyone's role later or **Remove** them. A device that drops
   off Wi-Fi rejoins automatically, with the same role.

If no address appears (common on a phone acting as hotspot), add it by hand:
your hotspot settings show it, often `192.168.43.1`.

## Running speeches

- **Start / Pause / Resume / Stop / Next →** control the clock. Tap a speech
  chip (PM, LO, …) to jump to it. Each speech is recorded automatically while
  **Auto-record** is on.
- The timer card changes shade as time runs out and names the phase:
  *Protected time*, *POIs open*, *Wrap up*, *Grace period*, *Overtime*
  (overtime flashes).
- **Ring bell / Ring ×2** ring on every device.
- **Flow notes:** type and press Enter or **Add note**; each note is stamped
  with the current speech time.

## Interruptions

- Debaters raise POIs and points from their phones. You can also raise one on
  someone's behalf: tap **POI / POO / POP**, then the seat.
- **POI:** the speaker accepts or declines (on their phone if they've joined,
  otherwise on yours). It expires after 10 seconds unanswered and closes after
  15 seconds once accepted. POIs are only possible in the open window, and only
  from the other side of the house.
- **POO / POP:** the clock pauses until you rule **Well taken**, **Not well
  taken** or **Under consideration** (or the point is withdrawn).
- If the debater raising a point has joined, their phone records it and sends
  the clip to you; the log shows *audio ✓*.

## After the round

Leave the timer to reach the round's review screen (also reachable from
**History** on Home):

- **Transcribe all**, or **Transcribe** on one recording. Tap a transcript line
  to play from there.
- **Ballot**: see [Ballots and reports](Ballots-and-reports.md). **Mark final**
  once there are no problems listed.
- **Export PDF**: Android opens the share sheet; Windows asks where to save.
  Toggle **Include transcripts** first if you want a shorter report.
