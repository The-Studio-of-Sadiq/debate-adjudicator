# Ballots and reports

Open a round's review screen → **Ballot**. Enter each speaker's name and score
(type it, or use − / +). Team results are **computed from the scores**, so the
ballot can never contradict itself.

## British Parliamentary

- Teams: Opening Government (PM, DPM), Opening Opposition (LO, DLO), Closing
  Government (MG, GW), Closing Opposition (MO, OW).
- Speaker scores 50–100 in whole points.
- Teams are ranked 1st–4th by total and get 3 / 2 / 1 / 0 points.

## UADC (Asian Parliamentary)

- Government: PM, DPM, GW and the Government Reply; Opposition: LO, DLO, OW and
  the Opposition Reply.
- Substantive speeches 70–80; reply speeches 35–40 in 0.5 steps.
- The team with the higher total wins; the margin is shown.

Ranges reflect common practice; tournaments vary, so tell us if yours differ.

## Marking final

**Mark final** is available only when nothing is listed as a problem:

- a speaker has no score,
- a score is outside the range or not on the step (e.g. 37.25 for a reply),
- two teams have the same total (adjust scores so the result is clear).

Changing a score afterwards reopens the ballot. **Comments for the debaters**
are included in the report.

## PDF report

Review screen → **Export PDF**. The report contains:

- motion, format and date;
- the ballot table (names, scores, totals, ranks or winner) and comments, or a
  note that the ballot isn't final;
- each delivered speech: time used and overtime, interruptions with outcomes,
  flow notes, and transcripts if **Include transcripts** is on.

On Android the share sheet opens (save to Files, send by WhatsApp or email);
on Windows you choose where to save it.
