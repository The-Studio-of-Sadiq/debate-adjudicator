Debate Adjudicator docs

Debate Adjudicator runs a debate room from the chair adjudicator's phone or laptop: timer and bells, devices joined over local Wi-Fi, interruption handling, recordings, transcription, ballots and a PDF report.

Guides

  • Chair guide: set up a round, invite devices, run speeches, rule on points, finish with transcripts, ballot and report.
  • Debater and timekeeper guide: join from your phone, raise POIs and points, keep time.
  • Transcription: offline models, cloud providers, what to expect.
  • Ballots and reports: scoring rules for BP, UADC and WSDC, and what goes in the PDF.
  • Troubleshooting: devices can't connect, bells are late, recording or scanning problems.

Contributing

  • Translating the app: add your language in one command, then translate a single file.
  • Adding a debate format: describe a format's speeches, timing, teams and scoring in one short file.
